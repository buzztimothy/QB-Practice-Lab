# P-001 — Suncoast clean master

P-001 defines a deterministic instructor/master accounting truth for the fictional Suncoast Home Services LLC for April 1–June 30, 2026. It contains no planted student errors and no P-002 behavior.

## Accounting boundaries

Customer invoices, receipts, applications, Undeposited Funds deposits, credit-card activity, and bank/card reconciliations use the P-000A operational service. Owner capital, bank-paid vendor expenses, equipment, the trailer deposit, and loan principal/interest are ordinary balanced general-ledger events because P-000A intentionally has no vendor, bill, fixed-asset, or debt subledger. Every financial result remains derived from journal lines.

The opening $8,750 A/R consists of three supported pre-April invoices. Their historical revenue is closed to opening equity before the case period. The legitimate December 31 CPA accrual is similarly closed to historical equity and paid before April 1. This produces the required April 1 assets of $97,700, liabilities of $33,450, and historical equity of $64,250 without a plug.

## Cash-basis limits

Cash P&L follows the documented P-000A behavior: invoice revenue is recognized on the date an applied customer receipt is received. Unpaid invoices and unapplied receipts are excluded. Deposits do not recognize revenue again. Expense-side activity retains posted treatment; this is not represented as a complete tax-basis conversion engine.

Cash revenue is $33,700 in April, $38,750 in May, and $43,000 in June. Natural accrual revenue is $24,950, $38,750, and $46,700 respectively. June accrual revenue exceeds cash revenue because the $2,275 Reynolds Invoice B and $1,425 Jenkins invoice remain open.
