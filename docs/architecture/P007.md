# P-007 — Month-end client meeting and financial explanation

P-007 adds the final Lab 1 client meeting as an attempt-owned domain layer above P-006. It does not build the polished readiness report. The meeting opens only when the latest P-006 close is `READY_FOR_FINAL_REVIEW`, the close evidence is still the latest assessment activity, no unresolved source-backed critical hook has appeared, the attempt identity matches, and a fresh comparison confirms the current books remain complete and reconciled. Every denial uses the same bounded error and reveals no scenario or issue count.

The immutable meeting package is calculated from the student's resolved operational attempt with the existing reporting engine. There is no presentation-only ledger or hardcoded report total. It contains April, May, and June cash-basis P&L summaries; deterministic changes; the June 30 Balance Sheet; Operating Checking; customer receivable/unapplied balances; and supported liabilities.

## Independent explanation and evidence

Michael opens only with “Okay. How did we do this month?” The student receives no required topics, outline, script, answer options, expected insight, or pre-performance coaching. The first non-empty explanation is stored exactly once with the financial-context snapshot, deterministic time/order, rubric version, independence state, dimension results, optional follow-ups, and instructor rationale. Resubmission cannot replace it.

`LAB1_READINESS_V1` evaluates five observable dimensions totaling ten points: Financial Accuracy 3, Relevance 2, Clarity 2, Business Insight 2, and Professional Communication 1. The deterministic evaluator recognizes complete financial claims and relationships against the context snapshot. Bare vocabulary does not constitute a claim. It accepts different structures and supported insights, including trends, receivables, cash/profit distinction, or debt context; it does not require every figure or an exact percentage. Material unsupported amounts, invented causation, cash-as-profit confusion, guarantees, and unsupported tax/legal conclusions reduce the applicable dimensions. It does not score verbosity, concision, grammar, personality, or emotional confidence.

Post-explanation help is append-only meeting chronology and cannot alter the original evidence's `INDEPENDENT` state, text, dimensions, or points. Optional revisions are not authoritative under version 1.

## Follow-ups and P-006 integration

At most two deterministic follow-ups are selected from supported subjects the student chose: profit/withdrawals, receivable concern, and debt concern. Michael asks a bounded business-owner question and never supplies an answer, conclusion, tax advice, legal advice, prediction, or benchmark. Student responses are preserved exactly but do not silently replace or rescore the first explanation.

Applying completed meeting evidence appends one `MONTH_END_EXPLANATION` observation to the P-006 aggregate and creates a new assessment snapshot. Earlier evidence and pre-P-007 snapshots remain frozen. The competency changes from `NOT_ASSESSED` to `ASSESSED`, `pointsAssessed` becomes 100, and its exact 0–10 result participates in the existing candidate bands. Accounting, critical-event, help-dependence, and required-review overrides remain authoritative; a ten-point explanation cannot bypass them.

## Isolation and secrecy

Meeting context, explanation evidence, follow-ups, and assessment integration must share one attempt. The ownership service resolves by authenticated student and attempt and returns not-found for a foreign combination. Reset preserves the historical meeting and starts the new attempt with no meeting until its own close gate is satisfied.

The student projection contains the financial package, neutral opening, their exact explanation, bounded follow-up prompts/responses, and post-performance help chronology. It excludes dimension/sub-point results, rationales, trigger kinds/rules, expected insights, ideal wording, and assessment mechanics. The authorized instructor projection retains the complete auditable evidence. P-007 adds no final report, badge, dashboard, production UI, model, deployment, QuickBooks integration, or Booked integration.
