# P-000A Operational Accounting and Reconciliation

P-000A adds an operational transaction layer without changing the P-000 financial source of truth. Customers, invoices, payments, applications, deposits, and reconciliations retain workflow identity and provenance; every financial event still posts balanced integer-cent journal lines. Applications and reconciliation selections are non-financial relationships and cannot create report totals or balancing plugs.

## Invoice, payment, and A/R lifecycle

Posting an invoice debits the attempt's Accounts Receivable account and credits its revenue line accounts. Receiving a customer payment debits its selected destination (normally Undeposited Funds) and credits Accounts Receivable. A payment application links a same-attempt, same-customer payment and invoice. Invoice open balance, payment unapplied balance, customer balance, and aging inputs are calculated from invoice lines and applications. The A/R control check compares that derived net customer receivable with journal lines posted to the A/R control account and reports any difference; it never repairs one.

Depositing received payments creates one journal entry that debits bank and credits Undeposited Funds. Deposit membership is explicit, attempt-scoped, and unique per payment, so the same receipt cannot be deposited twice. A deposit does not touch revenue.

## Reporting basis

Accrual P&L remains the P-000 journal-line projection: invoice revenue is recognized on the invoice date. Cash P&L removes invoice-source revenue from that projection and deterministically recognizes revenue from payment applications, allocated proportionally across the invoice's revenue lines with the final line absorbing integer-cent rounding. Unpaid invoice revenue is therefore excluded; unapplied receipts are not treated as revenue until application; depositing an already-received payment has no income effect.

This is deliberately limited cash-basis support for customer invoice/payment revenue. Non-invoice revenue and expense journal lines retain their posted treatment. P-000A is not a generic tax-basis conversion engine and does not implement bills, A/P timing conversions, inventory, payroll, depreciation, or tax logic.

## Reconciliation

A reconciliation records account ownership, statement dates and balances, cleared journal-line identities, and immutable line fingerprints. The calculated cleared balance uses debit-normal movement for bank assets and credit-normal movement for card liabilities. `balanced` is a calculation; `COMPLETED` is a separate state transition allowed only at zero difference with intact fingerprints. Uncleared ledger transactions remain legitimate outstanding items. Changing a previously selected line makes historical integrity false and prevents completion. PostgreSQL repeats account/attempt ownership and completion checks; no adjustment or plug is generated.

Credit-card purchases debit their legitimate expense or asset and credit the card liability. Card payments debit the liability and credit bank, so payment never duplicates the original expense. Bank and card statements use the same reconciliation model.

## Boundaries

Operational services accept identity only from authenticated server context and resolve all records through the owned attempt. Composite PostgreSQL keys and triggers reject cross-attempt, cross-customer, cross-template, duplicate-deposit, over-application, and reconciliation ownership violations. Student serialization omits instructor fields and reconciliation fingerprints. Template operational records remain immutable, while attempts contain all mutable bookkeeping state. Reset/retry is expected to copy permitted template operational records into the new generation and never mutate the template.

Statement truth is the externally supplied fictional statement balance and cleared-item set. Ledger truth is the journal-line register. Reconciliation compares them without bank feeds or QuickBooks integration; neither external service is necessary for deterministic practice cases.
