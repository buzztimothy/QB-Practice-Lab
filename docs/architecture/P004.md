P-004 adds an attempt-owned, deterministic conversation with Michael Carter. The student's exact authored text is retained; bounded intent recognition routes the message but has no authority to create facts, change accounting, unlock arbitrary evidence, or decide a bookkeeping treatment.

## Knowledge and response boundary

Michael's private knowledge is separate from student books, evidence documents, instructor interpretations, and clean-master truth. Each internal fact records its subject, authorized intent, document relationship, disclosure sufficiency, documentation expectation, and instructor interpretation. None of that model is included in the student projection.

Message handling has four explicit stages: deterministic intent recognition, server-side fact authorization, controlled client-language rendering, and allowlisted evidence authorization. ## Evidence, audit, isolation, and reset

Only allowlisted interactions can invoke P-003 unlock commands: an ABC document request unlocks only the ABC agreement, a payroll request unlocks only the payroll report, and an appropriate Visa inquiry completes only the personal clarification. Arbitrary or cross-attempt document and conversation identifiers fail closed. The personal clarification contains the factual personal purpose but no `Owner Draws` directive; the student retains responsibility for treatment.

Conversation messages and audit events are immutable and append-only. The internal audit captures exact student-message identity, recognized intent, authorized fact, response, evidence unlock, and client-message trigger. Student history shows senders, exact content, timestamps, and neutral conversation status, but not intents, facts, triggers, unlock rules, instructor interpretation, or future messages.

The service store boundary resolves attempts by authenticated student ownership. Unlocks and disclosures remain attempt-local. Reset preserves the old conversation/evidence history and derives a fresh generation with an empty conversation and Day 1 evidence state. Conversation never posts or changes journal lines, operational records, statements, reconciliations, P-002 transformations, or clean-master accounting.
