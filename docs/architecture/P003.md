# P-003 — Suncoast client evidence and document system

P-003 adds deterministic client evidence to each P-002 student attempt without changing the clean master or the attempt ledger. Structured evidence facts are canonical source material for this phase; no generated PDF is treated as accounting truth. Evidence links point to attempt-owned journal and operational records, while April–June bank and card statements remain independent external statement truth rather than values computed from a student's books.

## State and secrecy boundary

Each canonical document has one server-side state: `AVAILABLE_AT_START`, `LOCKED_REQUESTABLE`, `UNLOCKED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or `INSTRUCTOR_ONLY`. The student projection includes only available and unlocked documents. It does not include hidden document identifiers, hidden counts, states, unlock rules, instructor interpretations, scenario identifiers, expected resolutions, grading metadata, or critical-action definitions. Unknown, locked, unavailable, and instructor-only identifiers all fail with the same generic response.

Day 1 contains 23 visible documents: client information; April–June checking and Visa statements; loan support; Martinez, Reynolds, Jenkins, and Palm Breeze customer evidence; two distinct Home Depot receipts; one legitimate Sherwin-Williams receipt; pressure-washer support; CPA entry support; and the visible fact that a $750 receipt is unapplied. The Jenkins collectibility decision and future-job intent for the unapplied receipt remain unavailable. An instructor evidence map remains instructor-only.

Three documents are locked/requestable. Payroll support requires a matching prior payroll-support request and then exposes the exact P-001A component truth. The ABC agreement similarly requires its matching request and then reveals the trailer-deposit purpose. The June 24 personal-card clarification can unlock only after a distinct completed-client-clarification authorization. Requesting a document does not itself mutate accounting state or automatically reveal it.

## Audit, isolation, and reset

List, open, download, request, unlock, and evidence-reference navigation append deterministic immutable audit events. Evidence access is evaluated only inside the supplied attempt context; all operational links are remapped attempt identifiers. A different student's attempt has an independent inventory, unlock state, and audit stream.

Reset preserves the old attempt and its evidence history and derives a fresh generation with Day 1 visibility and all requestable documents locked. Evidence operations never post, repair, plug, or otherwise change journal lines, operational subledgers, reconciliations, or the immutable Suncoast clean master.

P-003 deliberately contains no conversation engine, coaching, scoring, final-report workflow, production UI, external deployment, or later-phase evidence.
