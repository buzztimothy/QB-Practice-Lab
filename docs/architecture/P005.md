# P-005 — Communication coaching and confidence foundation

P-005 wraps the attempt-owned P-004 interaction without replacing it. Student messages pass unchanged to the deterministic Michael Carter boundary, Michael responds first, and only then may an allowlisted rule create private BBB coaching. Coaching records are not conversation messages, evidence, accounting actions, or instructor case truth.

## Coaching authority and information horizon

The four non-numeric dimensions are `CLEAR`, `CONFIDENT`, `ACCURATE`, and `ACTIONABLE`; each record notes only a `STRENGTH` or `FOCUS` observation. These observations support coaching and later evidence collection, not a percentage, confidence score, readiness result, pass/fail outcome, or Client Ready status. Concision, reserved tone, ordinary courtesy, and harmless grammar are not detection rules. Appropriate uncertainty—such as verifying tax guidance with the CPA—is treated as professional strength.

Every record captures its attempt, conversation and student-message/draft reference, mode, help level, four dimensions, concise coaching content, timestamp, requested/viewed state, subsequent student action, and a private snapshot of the student's authorized information horizon. The snapshot contains only then-visible documents/messages and already-disclosed fact references. It is never included in the student projection.

Student-requested coaching can review a sent message or an exact unsent draft. Draft help never inserts text into Michael's conversation. Selective post-interaction coaching is limited to instructor-defined high-value patterns. Reflection is limited to deterministic meaningful behavior and can reinforce strong communication without manufacturing criticism. Coaching content uses reusable, case-safe language and never derives from clean-master answers or unavailable evidence.

Help levels are `HINT`, `DIRECTION`, and `WALKTHROUGH`. A hint asks a reflective question; direction identifies a stronger investigation approach; a walkthrough can provide example wording. None sends a message automatically. “Just tell me the answer” requests receive an explicit investigation boundary instead of an account, classification, hidden fact, or solution.

## Required situations and separation

COACH-01 through COACH-08 cover vague documentation, excessive self-undermining, acceptance of uncertain recollection, the CPA alteration boundary, appropriate tax deferral, unfinished P&L urgency, documentation pushback, and strong communication. Coaching may use the exact visible interaction but cannot alter Michael's response, invoke an evidence unlock, fabricate a client message, post or modify accounting, or weaken documentation requirements.

The ownership-enforcing service resolves coaching attempts by authenticated student and exposes student-requested sent-message and draft help. Coaching history and help usage remain attempt-local. Reset preserves the historical attempt and derives a fresh P-004 conversation, Day 1 evidence state, and empty coaching history.

## Future coach and SSI/LLM boundary

An authorized future coach projection may include original student text, Michael's response, coaching provided, help level, and subsequent communication. It does not yet implement a dashboard. A future bounded language model may analyze wording or render already-authorized coaching more naturally. Deterministic server code still selects the visible context, eligible interaction, help record, and hidden-data boundary. A model may not create facts, inspect undisclosed context, change Michael, authorize evidence, mutate accounting, or calculate readiness.

P-005 contains no final communication or confidence scoring, readiness outcome/report, pass/fail, hints outside the coaching record, production UI, external model, deployment, QuickBooks integration, or Booked integration.
