# P-006 — Client readiness assessment and evidence engine

P-006 adds an attempt-owned assessment domain above the immutable P-002 through P-005 evidence trail. It does not add a student report or new case interaction. The dependency direction is one way: accounting, document, conversation, and coaching events can produce immutable assessment observations; scores never rewrite those source events.

## Evidence before result

`AssessmentEvidence` records the attempt, competency, evidence type, source reference and source attempt, internal scenario relationship, severity, help state, outcome, self-correction/resolution state, deterministic order/time, and instructor explanation. Appending evidence rejects a source from another attempt and returns a deeply frozen replacement aggregate. An evaluation creates a new immutable `AssessmentSnapshot`; reassessment appends another snapshot and preserves every earlier observation and result.

The instructor projection retains observations, source references, rationale, critical events, help history, close attempts, accounting completion, and snapshots. The student projection intentionally contains only the high-level competency result, public category maximums, aggregate assessed points/classification, incomplete flag, and bounded close result. It omits scenario IDs and mappings, evidence/source IDs, instructor explanations, clean comparisons, critical-policy internals, help-dependence calculations, and hidden unresolved counts.

## Rubric and competency policy

The explicit rubric is `LAB1_READINESS_V1`. Its public architecture is Technical Bookkeeping 40, Investigation & Problem Solving 20, Professional Judgment 15, Client Communication 15, and Month-End Financial Explanation 10. Internal `SUN-L1-01` through `SUN-L1-20` mappings select a competency and non-equal relative contribution. Historical actions are independent from that mapping, so a future rubric can re-evaluate evidence without rewriting the evidence trail.

Scenario outcomes distinguish correct work, incorrect or inappropriate work, supported escalation, and legitimately unchanged records. For controls such as the two legitimate Home Depot purchases, Jenkins receivable, CPA entry, and Palm Breeze receipt, unchanged books earn nothing by themselves: an affirmative immutable investigation/judgment observation is required. The engine does not infer unrecorded behavior.

Help is `INDEPENDENT`, `HINT_USED`, `DIRECTION_USED`, or `WALKTHROUGH_USED`. Correct helped work retains positive evidence with decreasing independence credit; it is neither automatically failed nor zeroed. A majority-help pattern marks a competency help-dependent and can cap an otherwise Client Ready result at Client Ready With Support. Latest evidence can demonstrate progression, while earlier weakness remains immutable. Self-correction records initial error and later correction separately and preserves whether correction was independent or helped.

Communication evaluation uses only the P-005 observable `CLEAR`, `CONFIDENT`, `ACCURATE`, and `ACTIONABLE` observations, their chronology, and help state. It does not count words, apologies, grammar, messages, personality, or internal emotion. Professional uncertainty, direct evidence requests, boundaries under pressure, and actionable next steps remain observable conduct rather than a psychological confidence score.

Month-End Financial Explanation has a typed evidence extension point, but P-006 creates no such evidence. It therefore returns `NOT_ASSESSED`, with `earnedPoints: null`; the ten points are neither awarded nor deducted. Any required `NOT_ASSESSED` competency makes overall readiness `INCOMPLETE`, so P-006 cannot manufacture `CLIENT_READY`.

## Critical policy and close attempts

Critical client-harm evaluation is separate from ordinary points. Version 1 recognizes source-backed hooks for supported Home Depot removal, CPA history alteration, unsupported ABC guessing, Jenkins removal/write-off, arbitrary unapplied-funds disposition, forced reconciliation, Palm Breeze removal, and presentation of unverified statements as final. A hook without an actual source observation is `NOT_TRIGGERED`.

States are `TRIGGERED_UNRESOLVED`, `TRIGGERED_SELF_CORRECTED`, `TRIGGERED_AFTER_COACHING_CORRECTED`, and `NOT_TRIGGERED`. Correction never removes history. Under the version-1 fail-closed policy, unresolved client harm blocks readiness; a corrected critical event requires instructor review; repeated/help-dependent behavior remains available to later policy review. The classification order considers required-competency completeness, unresolved books and reconciliation, critical-event state, help dependence, and only then the numerical candidate bands (90/80/70).

Every “I’m Ready to Close the Books” action appends both a final-accounting-state observation and an immutable close record with sequence/time, unresolved-accounting flag, material evidence-request flag, reconciliation state, critical state, and bounded result: `NOT_READY`, `BLOCKED`, or `READY_FOR_FINAL_REVIEW`. Its student projection never enumerates hidden controls or exact unresolved counts.

## Accounting comparison, ownership, and reset

Accounting completion compares the attempt to the authoritative resolved P-002 state across Trial Balance, cash/accrual P&L, Balance Sheet, A/R control and customer balances, Undeposited Funds, Checking, Visa, Payroll Liabilities, current reconciliation, and historical reconciliation integrity. Comparison normalizes attempt-local identifiers, not financial values. The resolved baseline deliberately includes the legitimate $750 unapplied payment and the supported $286.43 Owner Draw classification rather than demanding byte equality with P-001A.

The application service resolves by authenticated student plus attempt and returns not-found for either a foreign student or client-supplied foreign attempt ID. Evidence references must name the same attempt. Reset creates a new generation with empty evidence, close history, and snapshots while preserving the old aggregate unchanged.

No month-end client meeting, final report, coach dashboard, production UI, model integration, deployment configuration, QuickBooks integration, or Booked integration is part of P-006.
