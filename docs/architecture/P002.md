Derivation remaps every account, journal, operational-record, and reconciliation identifier to the new attempt before applying controlled transformations. The clean master and its protected source records are retained only as instructor comparison truth and are never overwritten.

## Scenario and provenance boundary

Starting corruptions are balanced journal states, not stored report adjustments. A legitimate $750 unapplied customer amount credits A/R and debits checking, keeping the customer subledger and A/R control aligned.

## Audit, reset, and isolation

The P-002 evidence trail supports review, account changes, correction/void, application changes, document review, unresolved flags, historical alterations, reconciliation work and adjustment attempts, account consolidation, and self-correction. Protected hooks capture high-risk actions without computing a score. The safe audit projection omits hook identifiers.

Repeated derivation is deterministic. Different students receive equivalent but independently identified state. Reset marks the old generation reset and derives a fresh generation, preserving the old attempt and audit trail without carrying mutable bookkeeping into the new attempt.

P-002 contains no conversation engine, communication coaching, readiness scoring, final report, production UI, external deployment, or QuickBooks integration.
